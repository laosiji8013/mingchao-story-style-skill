# Humor Units

Humor in this skill is controlled narrator presence, not stand-up comedy.

> 纠正一个误区：幽默不是「可选的调味料」，是让叙事有呼吸的血肉。
> 完全没有幽默的说明文，骨架再对也是平淡的。但幽默必须「挂在事实上」，不能脱离事实单独存在。
> 可复用的包袱句式骨架见 `references/punchline-units.md`；本文件负责控制「什么时候用、用多轻」。

## 1. Humor Comes After Fact

Order:

```text
事实 -> 判断 -> 轻微旁白
```

Do not use humor to replace evidence.

## 2. Parenthetical Aside

Use a short aside to lower reading pressure.

Allowed:
- clarifying a practical limitation
- acknowledging awkwardness
- making a small everyday contrast

Avoid:
- long jokes
- personal mockery
- jokes about sensitive risk
- repeated catchphrases

## 3. Dry Contrast

The safest humor is often a dry contrast:

```text
听起来很高级，落到现场，其实就是别让人找不到东西。
```

This keeps humor tied to meaning.

## 4. Tone By Domain

AI/product:
- mild humor allowed
- avoid hype and sarcasm

Equipment/process:
- humor minimal
- responsibility and clarity first

History/person:
- humor allowed when it clarifies contradiction
- avoid turning suffering or death into entertainment

## 5. Humor Quality Gate

Remove the joke if:
- it can stand alone without the fact
- it weakens credibility
- it makes the reader remember the joke but forget the point
- it imitates a source catchphrase too closely
