# Rhetoric Patterns

## Length Control

Corpus measurements suggest:
- Paragraph median: about 37 Chinese characters without punctuation.
- Sentence median: about 31 Chinese characters without punctuation.

Use as execution defaults:
- Normal paragraph: 20-60 Chinese characters.
- Dense explanatory paragraph: up to 120 Chinese characters.
- Most paragraphs: 1-2 sentences.
- Break before important turns.

## Sentence Rhythm

Use:
- Short declarative sentences.
- One clean claim at a time.
- Commas for quick pacing, full stops for emphasis.
- Question marks only when they pull the reader into the problem.

Avoid:
- Multi-layer clauses.
- Long textbook definitions.
- Paragraphs that carry three or more claims.

## Plain-Language Translation

Before using a technical term repeatedly, translate it.

Internal pattern:

```text
所谓 X，不是 A，而是 B。
```

Polished pattern:

```text
这个词听起来复杂，其实说的是：{ordinary explanation}。
```

Examples:
- 多模态：不只看文字，也能看图、听声音、理解视频。
- Agent：不是只回答问题，而是能按步骤帮你做事。
- 工作流：把一件事拆成固定步骤，让人和机器按流程配合。
- 上下文窗口：模型一次能摊开处理的材料范围。

## Contrast Engine

Use contrast to create movement:

```text
看起来是 X，真正麻烦的是 Y。
```

Useful contrast pairs:
- 技术问题 / 使用问题
- 工具问题 / 流程问题
- 功能增加 / 责任变化
- 表面顺利 / 隐患扩大
- 个人能力 / 制度压力

## Human Aside

A controlled aside can make the voice human.

Use:
- Parentheses for a short aside.
- A mild comment after a clear fact.
- A small everyday comparison.

Avoid:
- Long jokes.
- Mockery.
- Humor before facts.
- Humor in serious safety, legal, or sensitive contexts.

## Judgment Phrases

Use original judgments with clear support:
- 关键不在 X，而在 Y。
- 这不是万能工具，它解决的是一类很具体的问题。
- 如果没有流程配合，再好的工具也只是摆设。
- 这一步看起来小，实际改的是责任边界。

Avoid unsupported absolutes:
- 必然成功
- 彻底改变
- 完全解决
- 没有任何问题

## Revision Checklist

Before final output:
- Is the first useful conclusion visible early?
- Does each paragraph carry one point?
- Are hard terms translated before being used?
- Does every turn come from facts?
- Is humor controlled?
- Does the ending close the point?
