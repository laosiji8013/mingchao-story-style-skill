# Chapter Rhythm

This file distills whole-book rhythm from full slicing. It contains no source passages.

## Macro Rhythm

The book operates through alternating modes:

```text
人物/事件进入 -> 背景补足 -> 压力累积 -> 转折出现 -> 判断解释 -> 局部收束 -> 下一事件进入
```

For non-history writing:

```text
对象进入 -> 使用场景 -> 痛点累积 -> 误区/限制出现 -> 机制解释 -> 价值边界 -> 下一步
```

## Emotional Curve（情绪曲线）

> 平淡的根因之一，是全文一个调子：从头到尾都在「平铺直叙地解释」。
> 好的叙事要有情绪起伏，让读者在「松—紧—松」之间被推着走。

全文字数按比例分配情绪阶段：

| 阶段 | 位置 | 干什么 | 用什么手法 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 开场钩子 | 0-15% | 一句话抓住人 | 场景开场 / 反常识判断 / 人物活化 |
| 松口气 | 15-30% | 抖个包袱，拉近距离 | 先抬后摔 / 先正经后反杀 |
| 压上去 | 30-60% | 问题变重，矛盾累积 | 压力模型 / 转折推进 |
| 反转 | 60-80% | 揭示真正的原因/边界 | 关键不在X而在Y / 反差 |
| 收束留钩子 | 80-100% | 金句收尾，留一句能转发的话 | 金句收束 / 数字锚点 |

执行要点：
- 短文的情绪曲线可以压缩，但「钩子 + 反转 + 收束」三段不能少。
- 严肃题材（装备/安全/合规）砍掉「松口气」阶段，但「钩子」和「收束」仍要保留——钩子用场景，收束用责任。

## Paragraph Rhythm

Use short paragraphs as default.

Recommended:
- 1 idea per paragraph
- 1-2 sentences per paragraph
- strong turn after several narrative steps
- local closure after explanation

## Decile-Level Reading

The full index groups slices into 10 position deciles. Agents can use `slice-index.csv` to study where functions cluster.

General use:
- Early sections need more orientation and reader relationship.
- Middle sections rely on event movement and reversals.
- Later sections need more closure and consequence.

## Scene-To-Explanation Ratio

Do not explain too early.

Preferred sequence:

```text
先让读者看到事情。
再告诉读者这件事为什么重要。
```

If writing a technical explainer, shorten the scene but do not remove it.

## Ending Discipline

Every section should leave the reader with one clear result:
- what changed
- what was misunderstood
- what boundary appeared
- what action follows

Avoid ending with:
- decorative emotion
- grand slogans
- source-like theatrical phrasing
