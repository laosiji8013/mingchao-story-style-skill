# Distillation Workflow

## Positioning

Use this workflow to turn a long source's expression method into callable, testable, transferable agent instructions.

## Seven Stages

### 1. Global Understanding

Output:
- Source positioning
- Narrative stance
- Reader relationship
- Expression goal
- Transfer boundary

### 2. Parallel Extraction

Extract five unit types:

| Unit | What to Extract |
|---|---|
| Structure | Opening, transition, ending, and paragraph movement. |
| Tone | Plain speech, judgment, restraint, and light humor. |
| Syntax | Short sentences, sentence breaks, parallel clauses. |
| Translation | How professional or abstract ideas become ordinary language. |
| Story | How people, conflict, contrast, and scenes move the text. |

### 3. Triple Validation

Keep a candidate style unit only when it passes all three checks:

1. Repetition: appears in at least two slices.
2. Transferability: works for new topics such as AI, products, equipment, or procedures.
3. Non-verbal-tic: is not just a fixed catchphrase or author-specific habit.

Delete units that fail.

### 4. RIA++ Construction

Record each style unit as:

```markdown
## Style Unit Name
- R: Source phenomenon summary without storing long copyrighted text
- I: Interpretation in your own words
- A1: Function in the source
- A2: Trigger scenario for transfer
- E: Execution steps
- B: Boundary and blind spots
```

### 5. Link Relationships

Track dependencies:
- "Conclusion first" depends on short sentence control.
- "Complex concept translation" depends on scene-based explanation.
- "Story movement" depends on conflict or contrast.
- "Light humor" must be constrained by fact priority.

### 6. Stress Test

Test the skill on:
- AI model explanation
- Product knowledge explanation
- Equipment or process explanation
- Topics likely to become report-like
- Topics likely to become over-humorous

### 7. Delivery

Final delivery should include:
- `SKILL.md`
- `references/style-profile.md`
- `references/structure-patterns.md`
- `references/rhetoric-patterns.md`
- `references/output-rules.md`
- `references/glossary.md`
- `references/examples.md`
- `references/anti-examples.md`
- `references/test-prompts.json`
- `references/handoff.md`
